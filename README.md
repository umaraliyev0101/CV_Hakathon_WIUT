# Showroom Pulse

Local customer-experience analytics for car showrooms. One showroom camera (an uploaded video or a
live webcam) becomes anonymous, aggregated statistics and evidence-backed recommendations for the
general manager and marketing lead. Built for the WIUT Hackathon 2026 CV final.
Sister project: Showroom Coach (per-salesperson coaching); the two merge into one product, see
[docs/INTEGRATION.md](docs/INTEGRATION.md). Pitch material: [docs/PITCH_NOTES.md](docs/PITCH_NOTES.md).

## The problem

A showroom knows how many cars it sold, not how many customers looked at a car, stayed, and walked
out without anyone coming to them. Camera products exist for footfall and dwell, but they stop at
generic zones. The question a dealer can act on is: **which car attracted interest that no
salesperson served, and when?**

## What it measures

- Footfall, groups, occupancy over time, peak occupancy, visit length.
- Per car: visitors who stopped, stop rate, median and total dwell, repeat visits, deep-interest
  visitors, share of that interest that got no salesperson, attention rank.
- Customer journeys (zone sequences such as Entrance, Car A, Car C, Desk) and the funnel
  entered, browsed, deep interest, assisted, reached desk.
- Unserved interest: a customer dwells at a car and no staff member comes near. Live mode raises an
  alert once the dwell passes a threshold.
- Recommendations (rules R1-R7). Each one carries its evidence numbers, a confidence from sample
  size, and links to the clip moments and contact sheets. Nothing acts on its own.
- Staff are separated from customers by auto-suggest plus one click (an imported `roles.json` from
  Showroom Coach also works). Staff are excluded from customer statistics.

## How it works

```
video file / webcam JPEG frames (/ws/live)
        |  sources: sample at 5 fps, max 960 px wide
        v
detect (YOLOX-s: person + car/truck/motorcycle/bus)  ->  pose (RTMPose-s, 17 keypoints)
        v
tracking (ByteTrack, supervision)  ->  appearance embedding per track (HSV + DINOv2-small)
        v
PERCEPTION CACHE  runs/cache/<key>/   (models run once per video)
        v                                   zones, roles, thresholds change below this line
identity.merge (tracks -> persons)  ->  timeline (foot point, metres, speed)
        v
zones (auto car zones from the keyframe, user-editable)  ->  journey (hysteresis)
        v
roles (suggest + one click) -> interest (assisted / unserved) -> stats -> insights R1-R7
        v
report: JSON, CSV, heatmap, flow, charts, face-blurred annotated mp4 + contact sheets
```

Editing a zone or flipping a role re-scores from the cache in seconds with no re-detection.
Everything runs on CPU through ONNX Runtime; no torch.

## Quickstart

Requires Python 3.12 and the model files in `models/` (they are not committed; `models/MANIFEST.json`
lists URL and sha256 for each).

```bash
./run.sh                      # creates .venv if missing, serves http://localhost:8000 (127.0.0.1 only)
.venv/bin/pulse analyze path/to/video.mp4 --out runs/demo --preset short
.venv/bin/pulse analyze path/to/video.mp4 --out runs/demo --preset dealership --zones zones.json --roles roles.json
.venv/bin/pulse eval --out docs/EVAL.md      # regenerate every number in docs/EVAL.md
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q                # default test suite
```

Presets: `short` (10-60 s clips) and `dealership` (long CCTV footage, up to 300 s are analysed per
job; longer videos are trimmed with a warning). The web flow is upload, progress, zones, staff
confirmation, results dashboard; `/live` is the webcam page. `PORT=9000 ./run.sh` changes the port.
For a LAN demo set `PULSE_ALLOWED_HOSTS=hostname1,hostname2`.

## Docker

A `Dockerfile` is provided (python:3.12-slim, non-root user, health check on `/api/health`). Models are
**not baked into the image**: they are large and each has a licence record in `models/MANIFEST.json`
that should stay with the files, so mount them at `/app/models`. Job data lives in the `/app/jobs` volume.

```bash
docker build -t showroom-pulse:local .
docker run --rm -p 127.0.0.1:8000:8000 -v "$PWD/models:/app/models" -v pulse-jobs:/app/jobs \
  -e PULSE_ALLOWED_HOSTS=localhost,127.0.0.1 showroom-pulse:local
```

Publish the port on 127.0.0.1 only (as above) unless a reverse proxy with TLS fronts it; add the
public hostname to `PULSE_ALLOWED_HOSTS`. `scripts/deploy.sh` is an on-prem systemd/nginx deployment
script that has never been run (it needs `--i-know-this-deploys` and Linux).

## Results

Source of truth: [docs/EVAL.md](docs/EVAL.md), generated by `pulse eval` (deterministic). The numbers
below are copied from its Targets table. They are measured on six indoor MEVA clips (school
hallways, lobbies, a stairwell, a gym), not on showrooms. Test = four clips, pooled; dev = two clips,
the only data the two identity-merge knobs were tuned on.

| metric | target | measured (test) | measured (dev) | met? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| entry/exit count error (pooled entries + exits) | <= 10% | 50.0% | 3.6% | NO |
| occupancy MAE (persons per second) | <= 0.5 | 4.02 | 1.00 | NO |
| zone dwell median error (Dealership preset: %) | <= 10% | 42.0% | 40.7% | NO |
| journey accuracy (1 - normalised edit distance) | >= 80% | 52.5% | 60.0% | NO |
| file mode real-time factor (CPU, 5 fps; 60 s 720p excerpt, then the six full 5-min clips) | >= 1.0x | 1.55x on the 60 s excerpt; full 5-min clips 0.88x-1.86x (below 1x: G421 03-13 0.88x, G299 03-09 0.92x) | (speed is not split by clip) | partly |
| live mode fps (engine step on frames with 1-4 people; live smoke paced / max, at most 2 people in view) | >= 5 fps | 8.27 engine; smoke 5.02 paced, 10.94 max | (not split by clip) | yes |

**Four accuracy targets are missed on test, and file-mode speed is only partly met. We report them
as measured; no target or threshold was lowered.** Why, in plain terms (details in EVAL.md):

- *Entry/exit and occupancy.* MEVA annotates only people who take part in an annotated activity.
  Where many real people walk through without one (G421: 2 annotated enters, 44 entries counted by
  us), the error is mostly un-annotated truth, not detector error. Crowded scenes (G421 lobby, G299
  gym, 320+ tracks) also fragment tracks. The pooled test figure is dominated by such clips and says
  little about showroom accuracy. On the two dev clips the error is 3.6%, but that is a fit to dev.
- *Dwell and journey.* MEVA ground-truth tracks are per activity and short (median about 1.2 s), so
  these two metrics measure zone assignment inside short snippets, not whole visits. They are
  invariant to the identity merge by construction and cannot detect merge errors. The zones used
  are agent-drawn and unreviewed.
- *Speed.* The 1.55x figure is a quiet 60 s excerpt; crowded 5-minute clips run below real time on
  this CPU configuration (G421 0.88x, G299 0.92x). Live mode meets 5 fps with at most 4 people in view.
- *Tracking.* Pooled test IDF1 is 19.7% (raw tracks) and 19.5% (merged persons); the merge does not
  improve IDF1 at the tuned threshold. GT ids are per activity, which penalises merging.
- *Showroom-domain evidence is thin.* The only showroom-like numbers are stop precision 2/2 and
  recall 3/6 (usable-window recall 2/2) on two stock auto-show clips with agent-made, unreviewed
  labels: an indication, not an accuracy estimate. Recommendation rules R1-R7 are checked on scripted
  scenarios (all fire or stay quiet as expected), not on real showroom outcomes.

Tuning disclosure: test numbers were visible during tuning (see EVAL.md Method); treat the tuned
`merge_sim_thr` 0.9 as a dev-set fit.

## Privacy

Showroom Pulse is built for anonymous, aggregated analytics; it is decision support only. The UI
banner reads: "Anonymous, aggregated analytics; decision support only."

- **No face recognition.** No face is ever matched, stored as an identity or used as a feature.
- **No sensitive inference.** No age, gender, emotion or ethnicity estimation. None of those models
  are in the repo (see the licence table: every model is a detector, pose, or generic appearance model).
- **No re-identification across visits.** The same person on another day, or after leaving, is a new
  anonymous visitor. Appearance embeddings only join track fragments inside one video or one live session.
- **Faces are blurred in every rendered output** (annotated video, contact sheets, thumbnails, the
  zone keyframe). The blur covers every *detected* person: raw detections down to score 0.2 plus the
  tracked boxes of the frame and one sampled frame either side. **A person the detector misses
  entirely cannot be blurred**, so share rendered outputs with that in mind. The raw keyframe stays
  only inside the local perception cache directory (`runs/cache/`).
- **The webcam check stores only a pixelated frame** (24 px wide, upscaled), so the operator's face is
  never stored legibly. **Live mode stores no frames**: JPEGs are decoded in memory, analysed and discarded.
- **On-premise.** Everything runs on the local machine; the server binds 127.0.0.1 and rejects
  foreign Host/Origin headers. No cloud calls, no accounts.
- **Retention.** Uploads and job data are deleted after 24 h by a cleanup on app start. The perception
  cache under `runs/cache/` is separate local working data that you manage.
- **Signage.** Customers must be informed by signage that camera analytics is in use. This is a legal
  requirement the operator is responsible for; the tool does not replace it.
- **Decision support only.** Staff appear only as the "assisted" signal. No per-person scoring;
  per-salesperson coaching is Showroom Coach's job.

## Limitations

- **Accuracy is unproven on showrooms.** There is no showroom ground truth for entries, occupancy,
  dwell or journeys; the evaluated clips are school scenes. Four accuracy targets are missed on test
  (see Results). Role detection, assistance and unserved interest are not evaluated at all on real data.
- **Stop detection** has been checked on two short stock clips with agent labels (2 predicted stops,
  6 labelled). Treat as an indication only.
- **Moving or cutting cameras.** Zone statistics use only stretches whose view matches the zone
  keyframe; on hand-held stock footage that was 24.2 s of 61.8 s. The dashboard shows the analysed share.
- **Crowds.** Dense scenes (hundreds of tracks) fragment identities and run below real time on CPU.
- **Distance is approximate.** Staff-nearby checks use pinhole back-projection from an assumed 1.70 m
  height and 70 degree field of view unless the floor is calibrated; a warning shows when most samples
  fall back to box height.
- **Blur covers detected persons only** (see Privacy). Rendered outputs of footage with
  undetected people may still show a face.
- **One camera per job**, no cross-camera tracking, no CRM or DMS integration, no accounts.
- **Floor calibration** (zone editor, "Calibrate floor": 4 clicked floor points with real X/Y in metres)
  gives metric positions, speeds and a top-down heatmap in file mode only; live mode ignores it. Accuracy
  depends on the clicked points and ignores lens distortion; points above the horizon are marked invalid.
- **Not built:** printable report, sales-CSV join (stretch items).
- **The perception cache is unblurred local data** (`runs/cache/` keeps the raw keyframe); protect the
  machine accordingly.
- **Webcam check not run in the final verification chain tonight:** the FaceTime camera returned black
  frames (lens cover or missing permission); the gate was not weakened and live mode was tested through
  a file-fed WebSocket.
