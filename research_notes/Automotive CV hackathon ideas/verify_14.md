## usedcar-10 BozorLens: live price index from windshield price sheets

### Takeaway
No product that OCRs windshield price sheets into an index turned up in two searches (EN, RU) and the local market is moving fast toward state/operator digitisation (plate-level entry logs, about 1,000 cameras, a Customs-led "Digital Car Market" in specification), which erodes the "offline data nobody has" moat. The core premise, that sellers at Sergeli put price sheets on windshields, was NOT confirmed by any source I could read. Scores: novelty 4, feasibility 3, demo 3, business 3, moat 3, local fit 4. Verdict: sharpen.

### Cited Findings
- EN search found only listing/auction-based used-car indices and valuation AIs, none using offline price sheets: J.D. Power Used Vehicle Price Index [JD Power via Vehicle Remarketing](https://www.vehicleremarket.com/news/jd-power-to-offer-used-vehicle-price-index) (S); CarlStreet RMV [AiThority](https://aithority.com/machine-learning/carlstreet-announces-ai-powered-used-car-price-index/) (S); Carvago + Czech Technical University valuation AI, about 2% average gap to actual selling price, uses photos [silicon.eu](https://silicon.eu/used-car-trade-vehicle-inspections-with-ai-9086.html) (S); the search tool said explicitly "None of these sources mention OCR of windshield price sheets".
- RU search ("price sheet on windshield at Sergeli") returned no confirmation that sellers display price+phone sheets on windshields; nearest hit was a shop listing for a windshield phone-number card (a product, not evidence of bazaar practice) [prom.uz](https://www.prom.uz/ads/avtovizitka-dlya-parkovki/) (S). A second RU query also found nothing on windshield pricing practice (S) [Spot tag page](https://www.spot.uz/ru/tag/авторынок%20сергели/).
- Sergeli, Spot 9 Jul 2026: 5,000-7,000 cars enter daily; parking capacity up 32% to 5,326; trading points 1,927 -> 2,689 on about 24 ha; QR code at entry, cars with state plates identified by cameras and plate-less cars registered by QR; software "Avtobozor" tracks entries, exits, free spaces and violations in real time; about 1,000 cameras; a mobile app to find a car by its parking spot (example: spot No. 178) is in testing; the article does not mention price sheets, price boards or an online listing of cars with prices. Fees: 13,000 UZS (covered) / 11,000 UZS (open) per weekday, 69,000-73,000 UZS at weekends; sellers say "up to 250-260k UZS per car" in costs and a car sitting a month adds about $100 to its price — [Spot](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/07/09/car-market/)
- Sergeli, Spot 11 Jun 2026: 7 sectors, automated entry/exit, e-parking, Face ID; "563 cameras with AI elements" in one passage and "more than 600" in another (internal conflict); thousands of trading spots "moved to digital format"; the software is internal management tooling, no online platform for listing cars — [Spot](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/06/11/car-market/)
- Sergeli, Spot 10 Jun 2026: capacity 5,400 cars of which 3,500 places are for sale; more than 1,800 auto-parts outlets; "more than 1,500 cars a day" use the entrances on Yangi Sergeli Street (scope differs from the 5,000-7,000 figure, [CONFLICT in scope]) — [Spot](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/06/10/auto-market/)

- Precedent for a bazaar price series exists in manual form: Anhor (26 Mar 2014) quotes Bibi.uz's weekly survey of Sergeli prices (used Malibu 88.5-100.3M soum vs 67.6-99.75M new; the source prices were in US dollars converted to soum; not stated whether asking or transaction prices) — [Anhor](https://anhor.uz/society/poderzhannie-chevrolet-malibu-stoyat-dorozhe-novih/); bibi.uz did not resolve (DNS error) when I fetched it, so I found no active successor.
- Sergeli sellers also post online: Telegram channel @sergeli_avtobozor ("Sergeli car bazaar", 779 subscribers) publishes used-car posts in Uzbek Latin transliteration ("Narxi" price, "Probekt" mileage, "Tel" phone), mostly priced in USD ("9500$", "21500$", some bare numbers such as "5500"), 3-5 photos per post; the page does not show whether photos include windshield sheets — [Telegram preview](https://t.me/s/sergeli_avtobozor). This is a small channel and does not prove bazaar sellers duplicate their ads online, but it shows prices circulate in USD and in mixed-script text, so a parser must handle "$", bare numbers, UZS and Cyrillic/Latin.
- Avtoelon homepage shows an "Official dealers" menu and a loan-calculator banner but no price estimator, price statistics or VIN-history feature — [avtoelon.uz](https://avtoelon.uz/); OLX.uz car listings have a "Все объявления / Бизнес / Частное" filter — [OLX.uz](https://www.olx.uz/transport/legkovye-avtomobili/)
- OCR stack: PaddleOCR-VL is Apache-2.0, "efficiently supports 109 languages" including Russian (Cyrillic) and handwritten text — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR-VL); PP-OCRv6 HF tags list English/Chinese only (Cyrillic coverage unconfirmed) [TEAMMATE tech_radar.md]; RF-DETR Nano 2.3 ms on a T4 TensorRT FP16 (Apache-2.0) [TEAMMATE] — [RF-DETR](https://github.com/roboflow/rf-detr)
- Used-car volume: 57,400 used cars sold in Oct 2025 (-5% YoY), 38,000 new — [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/11/18/electric-car-purchases-surge-as-overall-auto-sales-recover-in-uzbekistan); live listing counts 2026-10-10: OLX.uz 67,961 passenger cars, Avtoelon 29,497 [TEAMMATE uzbekistan_market.md]; auto-loan issuance 34.6 trn UZS Jan-Nov 2023 (mostly consumer loans, new/used split unknown) [TEAMMATE] — [Anhor](https://anhor.uz/news/cerr/)

### Inferences
- [CALC] Bazaar "for sale" places (3,500) are about 5% of OLX's 67,961 live car listings, so the offline pool is a minority by stock; whether it is a majority by turnover is unknown. The claim "offline asking prices are not online" is unverified: many bazaar sellers probably also post online (not checked).
- [INFERENCE] The operator already has plate-level in/out timestamps (cameras + QR), so "days-to-sell by hashed plate" is something the operator or Customs can produce better and without sweeps; BozorLens's moat is only its own archive, replicable by anyone who starts walking later.
- [INFERENCE] Asking price is not collateral value; ask-to-sale spread in a haggling bazaar is unknown, and days-to-sell from presence-days conflates "sold" with "went home".
- [CALC] Revenue ceiling: 15 banks x 3M UZS x 12 = 540M UZS (about $46k a year) before per-call fees; break-even is 1-2 clients because COGS is tiny (a 300-car sweep at $0.1 per the idea plus one collector's wage). Plausible B2B shape, small pool.
- [INFERENCE] The honest, judge-testable version is buyer-facing: point at a sheet, get "ask vs OLX/Avtoelon comparable" now; the index is a by-product.

### Gaps
- No source confirms windshield price sheets at Sergeli (the idea itself says "to verify on site"); no Uzbek-language query was possible; no photos could be inspected.
- No evidence that banks/insurers lack price data or would pay 3M UZS a month; no used-car loan share; no ask-vs-sold spread.
- Whether filming inside the bazaar is allowed (private operator, 1,000 cameras, new security regime) not checked; the Digital Car Market scope (price registry?) unknown.

## usedcar-11 YardAudit: dealer-yard stock audit with a dust clock

### Takeaway
Lender stock audits are an established, vendor-served workflow in the US (human floorplan-audit services, an app-based self-audit at NextGear, a June 2026 press release for "continuous VIN-level collateral assurance" with "AI driven VIN recognition", a 2016 OBD-dongle audit product, vendor blogs on drone VIN counts), and CV automation of that is plausible, but the headline "dust clock" has no support and is physically confounded by washing, rain and parking spot. Plate-only reconciliation will miss plate-less stock, and VIN reading needs close range, not a two-minute walk. No Uzbek floor-plan lending evidence. Scores: novelty 2, feasibility 3, demo 3, business 2, moat 2, local fit 3. Verdict: sharpen.

### Cited Findings
- Search (standard, one of the few that returned): human floorplan-audit services and newer automated claims exist — "Vehicle Inspectors" per-unit audits, AiM Verify (2017), AUTOMAP, Digital Floorplan Compliance (June 2026), and a vendor blog on drone daily VIN counts; the search summary found "none of these results show a startup doing AI verification of dealer lot photos against VIN plates specifically" (S) — [Vehicle Inspectors](https://vehicleinspectors.com/volume-inspections/floorplan-audits/); [AiM Verify](https://www.vehicleremarket.com/news/aim-releases-wholesale-floor-plan-audit-platform); [DFC press release](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/917680782/digital-floorplan-compliance-inc-launches-continuous-vin-level-collateral-assurance-for-automotive-floorplan-lending); [CallSphere drone blog](https://callsphere.ai/blog/nobody-has-walked-the-back-lot-since-march-a-drone-now-reconciles-540.md) (vendor blog, not read)
- Vehicle Inspectors (read): local inspector does a "Physical VIN match against your manifest, odometer reading, title and key status" with timestamped photo proof per unit; "Exception-flagged units (missing from lot, VIN mismatch) are reported within 24 hours of the visit"; full-lot audits in one visit; scheduled monthly/quarterly recurring audits; per-unit price by quote only; no client app described — [Vehicle Inspectors](https://vehicleinspectors.com/volume-inspections/floorplan-audits/)

- No source on dust accumulation as a stock-age clock: my attempt to read a PV-soiling reference (Wikipedia) returned 404; the claim is unsupported.
- Auto-loan issuance 34.6 trn UZS Jan-Nov 2023 [TEAMMATE] — [Anhor](https://anhor.uz/news/cerr/); no dealer floor-plan lending or dealer-count data found [TEAMMATE uzbekistan_market.md gaps].

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The lender's real risk is existence ("sold out of trust"), which plates/VINs answer; stock age is already known from the loan start date, so dust adds nothing a lender lacks, and dealers wash cars for sale photos. If judges ask "how do you calibrate across rain, wash, colour and Tashkent winter smog vs summer dust?", 15 volunteer cars will not answer it.
- [INFERENCE] Newly sold stock is often unplated until registration (not verified for Uzbek dealers), so a plate-first design fails exactly on dealer stock; VIN under the windshield needs <1 m and torch per car, so "two-minute yard film" is only true for plates.
- [CALC] $13 per audit (150,000 UZS) vs a bank employee doing a 50-car check in about an hour: payback is thin unless audits are frequent and lenders actually hold dealer lines (assumed by the idea).
- [INFERENCE] usedcar-11 and fleet-13 are the same pipeline (phone walk video -> plates/VINs -> reconcile with a list) with different payers; run one of them, not both.

### Gaps
- Only one of five search attempts on lot-audit CV returned (standard mode); the drone/CCTV vendors it surfaced were not read beyond press-release level, and Ravin AutoScan/Click-Ins lot use remains unchecked beyond teammate notes.
- No evidence Uzbek banks run dealer credit lines or pledge dealer stock; no price benchmark for an audit; Uzbek dealer plate practice unverified.

## usedcar-14 FlipperFinder: hidden-dealer detector from photo backgrounds

### Takeaway
"Same parking lot or driveway across listings" is a documented human red flag for curbstoners, and I found no platform that automates it (one extended search); OLX.uz already labels Business vs Private sellers, which doubles as free ground truth for validation. The idea is novel, buildable and demoable; the weak spots are payer (marketplaces with in-house ML), false positives on generic courtyards, and scraping terms. Scores: novelty 4, feasibility 4, demo 4, business 2, moat 3, local fit 3. Verdict: keep (improvement: prove it on OLX data with honest precision).

### Cited Findings
- Extended search returned consumer guides, not tools; the strongest signal named is the same phone number on many listings, with the caveat that some curbstoners use different numbers per listing; "photos taken in the same parking lot or driveway across different listings" is listed as a red flag, and the summarizer found "no source that describes a platform actually running image-location matching" (S) — [Medium guide](https://medium.com/@hichamfadel/how-to-spot-a-curbstoner-the-unlicensed-dealers-selling-cars-illegally-6cde9f3211e2); [Bumper](https://www.bumper.com/car-advice/buying/curbstoning-scam); [AutoHunterPro](https://www.autohunterpro.com/blog/curbstoning-detection-guide-2025) (page returned HTTP 402)
- CarSnipe (read): red flags are multiple vehicles from the same account, "Photos taken in the same parking lot or driveway across different listings", vague ownership history, title not in the seller's name, dealer-like phrasing; the article names no detection tools — [CarSnipe](https://carsnipe.com/blog/private-seller-vs-dealer-facebook-marketplace)
- Private Cars (US curated marketplace): "Automated and manual dealer-detection keeps brokers, flippers, and commercial resellers out"; ID verification, seller name must match title, human review of every listing; the page does not describe photo analysis — [Private Cars](https://privatecars.forsale/)
- Auto.ru (Yandex) already monetises hidden resellers with rule-based detection: the new paid-listing conditions apply automatically if you "posted listings for cars not registered to you for at least 90 days", or are logged into several personal accounts, or the seller name differs from the registered owner / GIBDD registration; "you will not be able to publish listings for free"; a seller can get free terms back after a check via support chat; there is a separate "Auto.ru for resellers" product — [Auto.ru help](https://yandex.ru/support/autoru/); no mention of photo-based detection. This shows (i) the revenue logic exists, (ii) the incumbent method is registry/account based, which Uzbek marketplaces cannot copy without an ownership registry feed.
- OLX.uz car list has a seller-type segmented filter "Все объявления / Бизнес / Частное"; cards show no seller label — [OLX.uz](https://www.olx.uz/transport/legkovye-avtomobili/); an OLX.uz service ad page shows a "Biznes" header label; Avtoelon has an "Официальные дилеры" section, no seller-type label on cards — [avtoelon.uz](https://avtoelon.uz/)
- Avito's tech blog first listing page had no post on reseller detection, duplicate-image detection or photo-background fraud (only the first of about 30 pages read) — [Habr Avito](https://habr.com/ru/companies/avito/articles/); OLX tech blog returned HTTP 403.
- Avtoelon launched in Tashkent 6 Dec 2018 as a free listing site (Kolesa Group); the launch article says nothing about AI moderation or dealer detection — [Forbes.kz](https://forbes.kz/articles/kolesakz_vyishli_na_ryinok_uzbekistana/amp); Kolesa Group "repelled 25,000 attempts of unfair placement of ads" (headline only, body unreadable) — [Astana Hub](https://astanahub.com/en/blog/kolesa-group-otrazili-25-000-popytok-nedobrosovestnykh-razmeshchenii-obiav); AI photo moderation figures (4M photos, 72k fakes) are search-summary only [TEAMMATE saturation_map.md] (S)
- LightGlue: code and pretrained weights Apache-2.0; supports SuperPoint, DISK, ALIKED, SIFT; "SuperPoint follows a different, restrictive license"; DISK Apache-2.0, ALIKED BSD-3-Clause — [LightGlue](https://github.com/cvg/LightGlue)
- Model licences: RF-DETR Apache-2.0; SAM 2.1 permissive; SAM 3 and DINOv3 "OK+cond" custom licences (commercial use allowed with conditions, gated weights) [TEAMMATE tech_radar.md] — [SAM 3](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3); [DINOv3 licence](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov3/blob/main/LICENSE.md)
- The Sergeli "saigak" crackdown concerns parts/service hustlers, not hidden car dealers, so it is no prevalence evidence — [Spot, 1 Jun 2026](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/06/01/car-market/)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Free validation recipe: OLX "Бизнес" ads are labelled dealers; measure what fraction of them cluster by background (recall proxy), then count "Частное" ads that join dealer clusters (hidden-dealer estimate). That gives a headline number judges can remember.
- [INFERENCE] False-positive risks: Soviet-era panel courtyards and generic fences repeat across unrelated owners; many private sellers use the same street or car park; dealers can vary backgrounds deliberately; phone-number/ad-count linking is cheaper and probably what platforms already use, so the incremental value of backgrounds must be measured against that baseline.
- [INFERENCE] Payer is weak: marketplaces have in-house ML and fraud teams; 5M UZS a month (about $420) is a rounding error; the consumer 5,000 UZS (about $0.42) per check needs listing access that platform terms probably prohibit.
- If LightGlue is used commercially, avoid SuperPoint weights; DISK, ALIKED (BSD-3) or XFeat (Apache-2.0 per [TEAMMATE verify_07.md]) are the clean choices.

### Gaps
- No prevalence data for dealers posing as private sellers on Uzbek classifieds; no Uzbek-language or RU search succeeded (RU curbstoning query refused).
- Scraping terms of OLX.uz/Avtoelon not read; accuracy of background matching on Tashkent courtyards is unmeasured.

## usedcar-19 TwinCheck: free hidden-accident hints from listing photos

### Takeaway
The general "photo-based repaint/panel-inconsistency flag" is already sold: VahanBazaar's AI Vahan Inspection (Rs 249) reads seller photos "for paint, panel, shut and body evidence" (vendor articles of Sept 2026, no accuracy published), Inspektlabs offers "detecting repainting or prior repair using visual cues", and Auto.ru flags "poor-quality repair" in listing photos. The specific twist, left-right ageing asymmetry of paired parts (lamps, mirrors, rims), is not claimed by those three, but UVeye holds a granted patent on symmetry-based deep-learning damage validation. Left-right lamp ageing is a weak, ground-truth-free cue and listing photos rarely show both lamps head-on. Scores: novelty 3, feasibility 3, demo 4, business 2, moat 2, local fit 3. Verdict: sharpen.

### Cited Findings

## insurer-11 WitnessHunt: Telegram bounties for dashcam witness footage

### Takeaway

## safety-6 ParkOK: one-photo parking legality check

## city-13 BumpRegistry: audit homemade speed bumps from dashcams

### Takeaway
The problem and legal hook are documented in Uzbekistan (unauthorized speed bumps banned by the Road Traffic Rules since 1 May 2022 per Podrobno; Bukhara has 10 bumps on a 426 m street), and no computer-vision speed-bump tool appears on GitHub or the App Store (only sensor and crowd-report apps). But monocular metric depth cannot resolve 5 cm vs 10 cm, the payer (khokimiyat) has no evidenced budget, and mahalla politics defend the bumps. Scores: novelty 4, feasibility 3, demo 3, business 2, moat 2, local fit 4. Verdict: sharpen.

### Cited Findings
- Podrobno (24 Jun 2022): Bukhara residents complain of bumps installed without control by mahalla households, "almost every 15-20 metres", one 426 m street with 10 bumps and a 467 m street with 7; ambulances and fire trucks must cross them; the article cites a state standard "Искусственные неровности. Общие технические требования. Порядок применения" and says the ПДД (April 2022, paragraph 4, effective 1 May 2022) prohibits "самовольная установка неуполномоченными юридическими и физическими лицами искусственных неровностей"; no official counts, no response from authorities, a mahalla chairman defends them; no digital/AI inventory — [Podrobno](https://podrobno.uz/cat/obchestvo/kto-prav-i-chto-delat-v-bukhare-zhaluyutsya-na-ogromnoe-kolichestvo-lezhachikh-politseyskikh-kotorye/)
- Search summary (RU): no norm or procedure for dismantling homemade bumps was found for Tashkent; Kun.uz reports a 2024 mass dismantling of unauthorized structures in Tashkent without saying bumps are included (S) — [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/69173020)
- Ready-made rubber/plastic bump modules sell on Tashkent's prom.uz: for example 5 cm x 35 cm modules, from 205,740 UZS per unit (24 offers from 10 sellers), no standard cited — [prom.uz](https://www.prom.uz/section/politseyskie-lezhachie/)
- Russian standard context (Wikipedia RU): dimensions depend on permitted speed, GOST R 52605-2006 is the technical standard, markings/signs per GOST R 52289/52290/51256, bumps allowed only where there is artificial lighting; many existing bumps violate dimensions, signs and markings; the page covers Russia only — [Wikipedia RU](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Искусственная_неровность)
- Municipal audits of traffic-calming devices exist but are manual and complaint-triggered: Philadelphia's City Controller looked at 140 speed cushions at schools and found "only two were installed properly", dimensions for 99% did not match the blueprint, about 95% were steeper than the allowed 10% grade; the review began after complaints, measurement method not stated in the article (WHYY, updated 17 Feb 2026) — [WHYY](https://whyy.org/articles/philadelphia-speed-bumps-traffic-cushions-report/); other manual audits from the same search (S): Bengaluru (33 obstacles to be flattened; an Indian Roads Congress member quoted saying at least 95% of the city's humps are illegal) [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/bbmp-bumps-more-humps-2554048); Ghana roads ministry audit committee [Graphic Online](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/roads-ministry-audits-speed-humps.html); Misurata, Libya study of 62 bumps with 66% out of specification [IJEIT](https://ijeit.misuratau.edu.ly/index.php/ijeit/article/view/153); UK legal limit for humps cited as 100 mm, Indian standard 10-12 cm; the search "didn't turn up anything about AI or dashcam tools for measuring speed bumps"
- GitHub topic speed-bump-detection has no repositories; topic speed-bump has 2 repos, both phone-sensor apps (BumpCompanion, Kotlin/TypeScript), no computer vision — [speed-bump-detection](https://github.com/topics/speed-bump-detection); [speed-bump](https://github.com/topics/speed-bump)
- App Store (US) probe "speed bump detector camera": Radarbot (96,782 ratings) and a "Speed Cameras Radar Detector" list speed-bump alerts from community/shared databases; no app uses camera-based detection — [iTunes API query](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=speed+bump+detector+camera&entity=software&limit=12&country=us)
- Bee Maps docs/homepage mention no speed-bump class — [Bee Maps docs](https://beemaps.com/docs/)
- MoGe-2 metric depth: Reld 15.7%, delta1 76.8 over 7 datasets — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02546); LightGlue/RF-DETR-Seg (mask AP 40.3, 3.4 ms on T4, Apache-2.0) [TEAMMATE tech_radar.md] — [RF-DETR](https://github.com/roboflow/rf-detr)

### Inferences
- [CALC, pinhole] For a 1080p dashcam with about 70 degree horizontal FOV (f about 1,371 px), a 5 cm hump at 8 m shifts its top edge by about 8.6 px and a 10 cm hump by about 17 px; separating them needs road-plane accuracy of about 2 px, which suspension pitch and slope will break; a 15.7% depth error is far coarser than the 5 cm effect.
- [INFERENCE] Detection (presence, paint, sign) is feasible; height needs a calibrated rig or coarse classes; IMU spike amplitude depends on speed and suspension (Cobalt vs Damas), so it can corroborate presence/severity but not measure height.
- [INFERENCE] The strongest local story is inventory + compliance + emergency routing (ambulances cross them), not "fleet avoid" routing, which cannot avoid residential-street bumps.
- [INFERENCE] Payer: a khokimiyat has to want to remove bumps its mahalla leaders defend; a pilot should start with an interested district and with emergency services.

### Gaps
- Six of eight search attempts for CV speed-bump detection/height estimation were refused (the RU Tashkent query and one EN municipal-audit query returned, the latter finding no AI/dashcam tool); academic CV/IMU bump detectors and Waze/Google Maps bump layers are not verified by me.
- The Uzbek standard's actual height/length/spacing limits were not retrieved (the Podrobno article gives only the title).

## city-16 FineFirewall: engineering-caused violation audit for camera sites

### Takeaway
The premise has official backing: the IIHS Philadelphia study found lengthening yellow by about one second cut red-light violations by 36%, and US DOT/NHTSA camera guidelines say shortened yellow raises violations and call for QA audits (summary only). Traffic engineers already audit signal performance from controller logs (UDOT/FHWA ATSPM). A video-only junction audit for fines was not found, but the payer is conflicted: Safe City is reportedly financed through fine-revenue sharing with a contractor (2019, search-summary only), the police gain little from fewer fines, and fleets face small per-fine stakes. The idea's data hook is also doubtful: a participant README says the elimination camera's signal head is "small, distant, and frequently occluded". Scores: novelty 4, feasibility 3, demo 3, business 2, moat 2, local fit 3. Verdict: sharpen.

### Cited Findings
- IIHS: lengthening yellow by about one second "reduced red light violations by 36%" (Philadelphia), "adding camera enforcement further cut red light running by another 96%"; yellow times matching ITE guidelines can reduce violations and crashes; the page gives no formula — [IIHS](https://www.iihs.org/topics/red-light-running)
- Search summary of the US DOT/NHTSA "Red Light Camera Systems Operational Guidelines": likelihood of running the red rises as yellow is shortened; lengthening yellow "within appropriate guidelines" significantly reduces inadvertent violations; quality-assurance audits of a random sample of violations by trained officers; warnings recommended after yellow changes (S; both PDF links returned HTTP 403 to my fetch) — [FHWA PDF](https://highways.dot.gov/media/11316); [NHTSA PDF](https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/documents/red_light_camera_systems_operational_guidelines.pdf)
- Same search: only prototype detectors found (YOLOv12 + ByteTrack + HSV signal state, YOLOv8 + SORT, YOLOv3 stop-line tool), no accuracy benchmarks for yellow handling, and "nothing directly addressed fairness or fine disparities across junctions" (S) — [example repo](https://github.com/HassanRasheed91/Red-Light-Violation-Detection); [example repo 2](https://github.com/anmspro/Traffic-Signal-Violation-Detection-System)
- GitHub topic red-light-violation has no repositories — [GitHub topic](https://github.com/topics/red-light-violation)
- ATSPM (UDOT open source, Apache-2.0): works from "the high-resolution data from signal controllers"; metrics named include vehicle volumes, arrivals on red, pedestrian delay, split failures; repo folders include AggregateYellowRedActivation and AggregateApproachSpeed; a 2012 standard defined controller event codes — [UDOT ATSPM](https://github.com/udotdevelopment/ATSPM); FHWA lists ATSPM under Every Day Counts with case studies (Utah, Georgia, Pennsylvania, Maricopa, Clark, Seminole, Portland) — [FHWA](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/arterial_mgmt/performance_measures.htm)
- Fine-appeal tooling elsewhere: RicorsoAI (Italy, 2026) reads fine PDFs/photos, flags formal defects (expired calibration, late notification, missing signage) and drafts appeals for EUR 5.99; it does not analyse camera evidence [TEAMMATE verify_08.md] — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/ricorsoai)
- Uzbek enforcement: AI pedestrian-yield cameras at 10 Tashkent crossings issued about 1,700 fines in 3 days, no appeal route or vendor named; owner-to-driver transfer since 1 Apr 2026 (fee 41,200 UZS); 50% discount if paid within 15 days, 30% on days 16-30; red light 3 BRV per the gov.uz-based table, 2 BRV per Oson Prava ([CONFLICT]) [TEAMMATE uzbekistan_market.md / verify_08.md] — [Spot](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2025/07/13/cameras-ai/); [Spot](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/04/01/driver-responsibility/); [Oson Prava](https://osonprava.uz/ru/blog/tablica-shtrafov-2026)
- Safe City is an Uzbek-Chinese contractor project in which fine revenue is shared with the contractor until the investment is repaid (2019) [TEAMMATE saturation_map.md] (S; my fetch of the Kun.uz article returned only the headline) — [Kun.uz 2019](https://kun.uz/en/news/2019/08/21/safe-city-project-implementation-in-tashkent-to-be-completed-in-2020)
- Elimination starter kit describes the sample data only as "Traffic events from a fixed road camera", sample videos unlabelled, no statement on signal-head visibility, resolution, or data-reuse rules — [Starter kit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/STARTER_KIT_README.md); the final brief states no data is provided by organisers (user brief)

### Inferences
- [CALC] A contested red-light fine at 3 BRV is 1.32M UZS (about $112); the idea's $20 per contested fine is about 18% of the full fine and about 36% of the early-payment price (660k UZS), so it pays only if the contest succeeds more than about one time in three and does not forfeit the discount (unknown).
- [INFERENCE] Perverse incentive: the operator earning from fines will not buy a tool that shows fines are engineering-caused; the more plausible payer is independent monitoring and evaluation of signal retiming (green-wave project) or a fleet owner choosing routes, not enforcement leadership.
- [INFERENCE] Yellow duration, red-entry timing histogram and dilemma-zone shares are measurable from calibrated fixed video; "sign visibility/sight distance" is not; "required yellow" needs approach speed (calibration) and the Uzbek minimum yellow rule, which I did not find.
- [CALC from standard ITE values, not fetched: perception-reaction 1.0 s, deceleration 3.05 m/s2 (10 ft/s2), level grade] y = t + v/(2a) gives about 3.3 s at 50 km/h, 3.7 s at 60 km/h, 4.2 s at 70 km/h; a 3 s yellow on a 60 km/h approach would therefore be a testable short-yellow finding if the Uzbek rule follows ITE (unverified).
- [INFERENCE] Using elimination sample videos conflicts in spirit with "no data provided" and with the elimination rule not to redistribute same-camera footage; declare provenance or shoot your own junction clip.

### Gaps
- Tashkent yellow durations and the local minimum-yellow standard were not found (search 12 returned only 2015-2017 items); no current (2026) evidence of wrongful camera fines or short-yellow complaints; the 2015-2017 items concern signal visibility, plate misreads and classification, not yellow length.
- Whether camera fine appeals accept private junction video; whether contesting forfeits the early-payment discount.
