# Saturation map: computer vision for automotive business and road safety (global + CIS/Central Asia), 2020 to Oct 2026

> Read-me. As of 2026-10-10. Method: about 110 web searches (English, Russian, Chinese; no Uzbek-language queries) plus about 120 page fetches. The shared web-search quota ran out mid-run, so some probes were cut (see Gaps). Tag **(S)** = claim taken from a search-result summary and the page itself was not read (blocked, 403/429, or not fetched): lower confidence. Untagged = page was fetched and read. Vendor figures are vendor claims unless stated. "Inferences" are my judgment, not sourced facts. Saturation scale used in Inferences: CROWDED (many funded vendors/incumbents plus open-source clones), CONTESTED (a few funded vendors or fresh 2025-26 entrants), THIN (only patents, papers, pilots or one niche vendor).

## Key Question 1: Vehicle inspection, claims, used-car appraisal and listing photos, vehicle history, tyres, glass, paint/body. Who is famous or crowded, and what are the stock hackathon versions?

### Takeaway
Phone-photo and drive-through damage inspection for insurers, fleets, rental and remarketing is a mature, funded and consolidating category (Tractable, UVeye, ProovStation, Ravin, Inspektlabs, Click-Ins, Self Inspection, plus AI embedded in CCC and Solera), so "YOLO finds dents on a photo" is a cliche. The gaps are repaint/repair-history detection, Uzbek/Kazakh deployments, and claim-photo authenticity (AI-faked damage) tuned to local fraud; I found no product for the first (Auto.ru only flags "poor-quality repairs" in listing photos), and no Uzbek or Kazakh vendor for the other two (Russia has Platforma and Auto.ru).

#### 1F. Paint / body (repair history)
- Established method is the handheld paint-thickness gauge (factory ~80-150 microns; >200 suggests repaint); gauges struggle with aluminium/plastic and well-blended repairs (S). ### Inferences
- CROWDED (avoid as a headline idea): phone/360 damage detection for insurers, fleets, rental and remarketing (>12 named vendors, 90 startups on Tracxn, incumbents embedding it: CCC AI about 11% of revenue, Solera Qapter); drive-through gantries (UVeye, ProovStation, DeGould, Ravin Autoscan, carpodX); dealer photo/360 merchandising (Spyne, Impel, Glo3D); tyre tread/DOT scanning (Anyline, WheelRight, ProovStation, UVeye); vehicle history (Carfax, Avtocod/Autoteka/Avito are data moats).
