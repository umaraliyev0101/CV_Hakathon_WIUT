## 1. Who organises and sponsors WIUT Hackathon 26, and which partners are automotive, insurer, fleet/taxi/delivery, telecom, bank or government?

## 2. Judging criteria and weights, prizes, final-round format and duration, and the elimination/qualifying task

### Takeaway
Final pitches are scored 25% Innovation & Originality, 25% Technical Complexity, 25% Impact & Practicality, 15% Presentation, 10% Business Potential, by a separate panel per track; 20 teams per track pitch 7 min + 3 min Q&A on Sunday 11 Oct after only about 8.5 scheduled on-campus hacking hours, code freeze 13:00. The elimination task was fixed-camera traffic-event detection (14 classes) plus accident anticipation, scored 60% hidden-test model, 25% public website with live upload demo, 15% code quality.

**Final brief vs elimination rules**
- The final-round brief (supplied by the user, no public URL found): each finalist builds a working AI product and defends it as a business case; no dataset or leaderboard; Mode "startup"; Track CV = images/video into a decision or action for automotive business or road safety; state where inference runs, speed on own hardware, cost per camera/inspection, quality at night/rain/local vehicles and plates; public deployed web demo that judges can open on laptop/phone; allowed: any LLM, coding assistant, open-source model, pretrained weights, public dataset or public API, paid services at own expense listed in the cost model — (source: user-pasted brief)

**Elimination task (organisers' PDF, mirrored in a participant repo)**
- Title "WIUT Hackathon 2026 - Computer Vision Track: Elimination Task": watch a fixed road camera, report every traffic event as a time segment with a class, and raise an alarm before an accident; one angle, no camera motion; unlabeled sample videos; hidden test set from the same camera annotated by organisers; run offline on organisers' machine — [Elimination PDF copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/WIUT%20Hackathon%20_%20CV%20Track%20Elimination%20Task.pdf)
- Part A (mandatory) returns [[start_sec, end_sec, label]] per .mp4; Part B (bonus) is a causal RiskEstimator.step(frame, t_sec) giving P(accident starts within 5 s) — [Elimination PDF copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/WIUT%20Hackathon%20_%20CV%20Track%20Elimination%20Task.pdf); [Starter kit README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/STARTER_KIT_README.md)
- 14 official classes: accident, near_miss, red_light, wrong_way, illegal_u_turn, stopped_vehicle (10 s+, not in a signal queue), jaywalking, failure_to_yield, illegal_turn, solid_line_crossing, stop_line, congestion, road_obstacle, fire_smoke; "Speeding is not in the list: it cannot be measured from this camera without calibration" — [Elimination PDF copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/WIUT%20Hackathon%20_%20CV%20Track%20Elimination%20Task.pdf)
- Scoring: Score_A = macro F1 over classes at temporal-IoU 0.3/0.5/0.7; Score_B = 0.4*AP + 0.4*F1_alarm + 0.2*mTTA/W; Model M = 0.7*A + 0.3*B; Elimination = 0.6*M + 0.25*Website + 0.15*Code — [Elimination PDF copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/WIUT%20Hackathon%20_%20CV%20Track%20Elimination%20Task.pdf)
- Limits: one 16 GB T4-class GPU, 8 CPU cores, 32 GB RAM, no internet, at most 3x video duration wall-clock for A+B (over budget scores empty), weights up to 5 GB, Python 3.10+ — [Elimination PDF copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/WIUT%20Hackathon%20_%20CV%20Track%20Elimination%20Task.pdf)
- Deliverables: public repo with one-command entry; public team website (team, approach, EDA, annotated sample results, live upload demo accepting ~2 min clips, one-page report). Website rubric: live demo 30%, sample-video visualisations 20%, EDA 15%, approach/report 15%, team/portfolio 10%, design/UX/extras 10%. Code rubric: runs as submitted 40%, reproducibility 25%, structure 20%, engineering judgement 15% — [Elimination PDF copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/WIUT%20Hackathon%20_%20CV%20Track%20Elimination%20Task.pdf)
- Extra-credit hints that reveal the imagined end user: "A dashboard view: events per hour, per lane, per class, as an operator would see it", demo on live stream/webcam, ablations, and "anything else ... an organizer or a city traffic centre would want to see" — [Elimination PDF copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/WIUT%20Hackathon%20_%20CV%20Track%20Elimination%20Task.pdf)
- Allowed public datasets named as examples: DoTA, CCD, DAD, CADP, UCF-Crime RoadAccidents, UA-DETRAC, BDD100K; open VLMs (Qwen-VL, InternVL) also named; do not scrape same-camera footage — [Elimination PDF copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bobomurodovd44/traffic-vision/master/WIUT%20Hackathon%20_%20CV%20Track%20Elimination%20Task.pdf)
- Camera/data per participants: 4K clips from a fixed camera at a Tashkent junction the organisers filmed; four samples (C3896 340 s, C3897 318 s, C3902 318 s, C3905 128 s); 10-bit 4:2:2 H.264 at ~140 Mbit/s (CPU-only decode, a bottleneck on 2-vCPU Colab); noon-to-dusk lighting; signal heads, zebra crossings, median — [PariVision README](https://github.com/baytoooo/parivision-traffic); [IntDev README](https://github.com/bakhti69/IntDev)

### Inferences
- Anyone who reviewed the elimination has seen "YOLO + tracker + rules on a fixed camera plus live-upload demo" many times; repeating it is the lowest-originality path against a 25% Innovation & Originality weight.
- With roughly 8.5 on-campus hours, 7+3 minute pitches, and 20 pitches in four hours, scope must be a thin vertical slice with a pre-staged demo; a deployed, phone-friendly demo is consistent with the elimination's live-demo weight (30% of the website score) and the final brief's public-demo requirement.
- Business Potential is only 10%, yet the brief says "defend it as a business case"; the engineering-constraint items (inference location, speed, cost per camera/inspection, night/rain/local plates) fit best under Technical Complexity and Impact & Practicality (75% combined with Innovation).
- The final brief allows paid public APIs (if costed) where the elimination forbade them, so unit economics must include any API cost per inspection.
- The organisers' "operator dashboard / city traffic centre" hints show what the CV-track designers imagine as the buyer; the final brief widens this to dealers, service centres, insurers, fleets and drivers.

## 3. Winning projects of earlier WIUT hackathons and of 2024-2026 Uzbekistan / Central Asia AI and startup competitions in automotive, mobility, insurance or road safety

### Takeaway
There is no earlier "WIUT Hackathon" (this edition is billed as the first inter-university one); WIUT/InnoWIUT's prior events are non-automotive. ## 4. Uzbek and Central Asian startups or companies already selling computer-vision products for cars, roads, parking, insurance or fleets

### Takeaway
Local CV supply for mobility is thin: state-led city-camera AI (Tashkent traffic monitoring centre; planned AI analysis of street cameras nationwide), ANPR parking operators and integrators (StreetPark Systems/Poytaxt Parking, ATTO, imported vendors), Kazakhstan's Sergek and Car ID/AI Parking, and Kyrgyzstan's state Avtouragan.
