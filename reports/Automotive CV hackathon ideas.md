# Automotive CV hackathon ideas

**How to read this.** Assumptions: Tashkent finals, 20 CV teams, 7-minute pitch plus 3-minute Q&A after about 8.5 scheduled on-campus hacking hours, code freeze 13:00 Sunday [1]; jury weights Innovation 25%, Technical Complexity 25%, Impact & Practicality 25%, Presentation 15%, Business 10% [2]; judges open each demo on their own phones. Method: 10 generators produced 168 raw ideas, 143 after de-duplication; web-searching skeptics checked all 143, killed 41, kept or sharpened 102, left 0 unverified. Below: 91 ideas (two near-duplicate pairs merged); 9 famous ones moved to the avoid list. Score = novelty×2 + feasibility×1.5 + demo×1.5 + business×1.5 + moat×1.25 + local fit×1, scaled to 100, minus 15 if famous; range here 49-75. $1 ≈ 11,800 UZS [3].

## Top 12 picks for this jury

- **AutodromRef** (75) — One overhead phone scores a driving school's practice yard on the exam manoeuvres (hill-start rollback, box reverse, parallel parking, serpentine) and saves replay clips. **Demo:** judge pushes a toy car round a printed A3 track under their propped phone; cone hits and rollback appear live. **Why it wins:** the practical exam is sensor-scored on a 100-point scale with a 1.2M UZS retake [4]; graduates' results carry 30 of 100 school-rating points [5][6]; exam-grade video tracks exist in India [7], no cheap practice tool was found; moat = replay library calibrated to real exam outcomes. **Payer:** driving schools, ~400k UZS per track-camera/month, repaid by 2-4 extra practice hours sold at 100-250k UZS.
- **CamAudit** (70) — Pre-trial rehearsal for road-camera AI: replays an integrator's clips under synthetic night, rain, dust and plate smear and scores them against Cabinet Resolution 148 (≥95% of plates captured day and night, use suspended at ≥5% data errors, 10-day no-fine trial, "error" undefined) [8]. **Demo:** judge drags night and rain sliders on a Tashkent junction frame, watches a plate reader fail, downloads the audit. **Why it wins:** extends the elimination task but sells the exam, not the student; vendors self-claim 97-99% on Uzbek plates [9] and no independent stress-test product was found; moat = local benchmark with a real-vs-synthetic calibration curve. **Payer:** integrators facing the trial, 15-40M UZS per audit.
- **Ko'pik** (71) — A technician films each soaped gas fitting; the app proves every joint was covered, spots growing bubbles and files a time-stamped "test witnessed" record against the cylinder serial. **Demo:** judge blows through a straw at a plastic fitting; the leaking joint lights up on a coverage map. **Why it wins:** Resolution 315 routes cylinder status into Eco-Himoya and U-Gas but says nothing about leak tests [10]; 33.9% of fuel-typed Avtoelon listings are petrol-gas [11]; Duke Energy's bubble patent targets pipes, not vehicle joint coverage [12]; moat = calibrated minimum-detectable-leak data plus workshop histories. **Payer:** conversion workshops and inspection centres, ~150k UZS per bay-month.
- **Quvvat** (69) — Buyer or lender photographs dashboard and DC-charger screen before and after a 15-minute charge; an energy balance returns a battery-health band (≥90, 80-90, <80%) and a price or loan-to-value call; Yorliq's nameplate OCR supplies nominal kWh. **Demo:** judge uploads our photo pairs and sees the band plus a listing-mismatch flag. **Why it wins:** 122.7k EVs are registered, 72% in Tashkent [13]; 99.2% of H1-2026 EV imports are Chinese [14] while OBD apps like EVScanner skip BYD [15]; BYD Royal Motors sponsors, MKBANK partners [2]; moat = model × charger calibration table. **Payer:** dealers, avtopodbor firms, EV-loan banks, ~30k UZS per check.
- **Be-Seen** (67) — A flash and no-flash photo pair isolates retroreflection and grades a courier moped's reflectors and tape before night shifts, with a fix list. **Demo:** judge photographs their own vest, bike reflector or number plate with and without flash; the grade appears in-browser in about a second. **Why it wins:** Yandex says 92% of 23,785 two-wheeler violations in 2026 were reflectors, lights, tint or passengers, about 4.7bn UZS in fines [16]; existing retroreflectometers are dedicated hardware [17]; moat = per-phone flash calibration and depot compliance history. **Payer:** delivery platforms and fleets, 3,000 UZS per audit or 150k UZS per depot-month.
- **PledgePulse** (66) — Banks send borrowers a Telegram link for a 30-second randomized challenge video of the pledged car; AI checks plate, VIN, odometer and replay attacks and returns a collateral flag. **Demo:** judge completes a random challenge on any car or toy, then films a laptop screen and sees the replay caught. **Why it wins:** Truepic and DoxAI sell one-off lender asset verification [18][19], no monthly re-verification product was found; auto loans reached 34.6 trn UZS in Jan-Nov 2023 [20]; MKBANK, Uzcard and the Central Bank are partners [2]; moat = per-car baselines and a published red-team table. **Payer:** lessors and banks, ~6,000 UZS per monthly check.
- **GapGauge** (64) — Hold any bank card beside a panel seam; card homography gives millimetres, ten stations give taper, and the mirror-side comparison flags likely repair. **Demo:** judge presses their own Uzcard or Humo card to a car or laptop seam and checks the number with a ruler. **Why it wins:** an ID-1 card is 85.60 × 53.98 mm [21]; Scope's 2019 beta and India's AI Vahan claim gap checks without a reference object or published accuracy [22][23]; moat = feeler-gauge-validated tolerance bands per Chevrolet and BYD model. **Payer:** avtopodbor firms and trade-in desks, 10,000 UZS per car or 150k UZS per seat-month.
- **Navbat** (70) — Station clips from drivers and dashcams become a live methane map: queue count, metres, open or closed, and throughput from successive time-stamped reports, telling taxis where to fill now. **Demo:** judge films a queue video on a laptop and gets count, status and "go to station B" on a map. **Why it wins:** on 25 Dec 2025 only 11 of 33 CNG stations serving Tashkent public transport were open [24]; state UGaz has no driver-facing feature [25]; moat = per-station hourly throughput priors and winter history. **Payer:** taxi and courier fleets, ~15k UZS per car-month; 576k taxi drivers were registered self-employed in 2024 [26].
- **TruckProof** (69) — Forwarders request a 20-second challenge video; AI matches plate, tech passport, vehicle type, driver and a fresh code before a load is booked. **Demo:** judge films their own car, plate and the random code and gets matched or failed checks. **Why it wins:** Overhaul and KYT verify photos or licences, not live plate-document-vehicle triangulation [27]; liveness is outsourced to MyID, which reports 19M+ users [28]; moat = shared verified-carrier graph and fraud blacklist. **Payer:** forwarders and Telegram freight marketplaces, $0.50 per verification.
- **FlipperFinder** (68) — Masks out the car and matches listing-photo backgrounds (walls, gates, garages) to expose "private sellers" who are dealer rings. **Demo:** judge pastes five listing photos and sees shared backgrounds and the seller-cluster graph. **Why it wins:** the same-parking-lot tell is a known human red flag [29] and Auto.ru already moves hidden resellers to paid terms [30], yet no automated matcher was found; OLX.uz's Business filter gives free ground truth [31]; moat = a seller graph that accumulates. **Payer:** marketplaces, ~5M UZS per month pilot plus a share of recovered dealer fees.
- **WashScan** (66) — A car-wash attendant circles the clean car with a phone; live gates check coverage, blur and glare and store a plate-bound, dated condition baseline. **Demo:** judge circles any object and watches the coverage ring close. **Why it wins:** wash-damage vendors sell fixed cameras at $23-63 per camera-month [32]; this needs no hardware and resells baselines to KASKO insurers, who signed 237,405 contracts in 2025 [33]; moat = wash network plus dated baseline library. **Payer:** car washes first for claim defence, then insurers at ~5,000 UZS per accepted baseline.
- **Drip-ID** (66) — Used-car drip test: a printed calibrated card under the car and two photos ten minutes apart separate evaporating A/C water from oil, ATF or coolant. **Demo:** judge drips water or tea on our card and gets the fluid class and a one-page verdict. **Why it wins:** a Tashkent avtopodbor from 300,000 UZS lists no leak check [34]; fleet photo tools exist [35] but no consumer drip card was found; moat = aged-leak library per local engine. **Payer:** avtopodbor inspectors and buyers, $1-2 per report plus card consumables.

## All ideas by customer

### Used cars, dealers & lenders

- **Quvvat** (69) — see Top picks.
- **Sotiladi Scout** (67) — Walk a market or lot with a phone; on-device OCR reads for-sale notes behind windshields, identifies the car, ranks a tap-to-call list by margin. **Payer:** dealers and flippers, ~$30/month or $0.30 per lead; 52-57k used deals a month [36][37]. **CV → action:** RF-DETR note detector → PP-OCRv6 phone and price → SigLIP 2 model ID → margin vs Avtoelon/OLX asks → call list. **Data:** 500 self-collected sign frames; synthetic notes on car photos. **Demo:** judge points a phone at printed windshield props. **Runs:** phone WebGPU; numbers stay on-device. **Moat:** map of unlisted supply with call outcomes. **vs existing:** listing scrapers → sellers who never list; measure notes per hour first; database rules apply [38].
- **GapGauge** (64) — see Top picks.
- **BoltSeal** (63) — Evidence-grade fastener log: guided macro photos of hinge and fender bolts flag wrench marks and cracked paint seals, abstaining on bolts legitimately removed on that model. **Payer:** trade-in desks and lenders, ~15,000 UZS per car (avtopodbor starts at 300,000 UZS [34]). **CV → action:** RF-DETR-Nano bolts → DINOv3-S sealed/disturbed classifier → left-right twin-bolt control → per-panel "removed?" log. **Data:** controlled before/after wrench test on scrap panels of 2 models; no inpainted positives. **Demo:** judge scans six bolts on our board, three secretly wrenched. **Runs:** ONNX in browser, server fallback (2.3 ms on T4 [39]). **Moat:** per-model factory-adjustment maps. **vs existing:** manual bolt checks [40] → first fastener-level AI found.
- **Columbo** (63) — Telegram and web chat asks the seller for the most informative next photo, running measured modules (GapGauge, RepaintRadar) until it says buy, walk away or inspect on site. **Payer:** regional buyers and dealers, 25,000 UZS per case vs a 300,000 UZS avtopodbor plus travel [34]. **CV → action:** VLM planner calls modules as tools → Bayesian belief → expected-information-gain next shot → advisory verdict. **Data:** inspector likelihoods, 50 team cases, scripted sellers. **Demo:** judge plays seller from our photo folder and watches each request. **Runs:** server, ~$0.01 per case. **Moat:** policy improves per conversation. **vs existing:** Tractable's guided video can ask for extra shots [41] → buyer-side agent; prove fewer photos than a fixed 12-shot protocol.
- **Proof-of-Inspection** (63) — Audits the inspector, not the car: live-captured avtopodbor video is checked for required steps (VIN, odometer, gauge contact, scanner plugged), reused footage and VIN plate vs windshield vs tech passport, then certified. **Payer:** banks, leasing firms and marketplaces that rely on inspection PDFs, 10,000 UZS per audit. **CV → action:** Grounded-SAM-2 + Qwen3-VL-8B step checks → video fingerprints → server-side hash → certificate; text-vs-frame contradictions flagged, never judged. **Data:** scripted staged inspections with omissions. **Demo:** judge records a toy-car walk-around, types "no damage", gets challenged. **Runs:** server ~40 s, <$0.05. **Moat:** inspector reputation graph. **vs existing:** Inspektlabs checks its own capturer's media [42] → third-party inspectors.
- **Lowside** (63) — Zone-by-zone scuff map for used mopeds and motorcycles (lever tips, bar-ends, pegs, cases, exhaust) plus bar and fork symmetry and part-age mismatch; no "slid vs dropped" verdict. **Payer:** moped lessors and courier fleets at check-in/check-out, ~$2 per check. **CV → action:** SAM 3 contact-point crops → DINOv3 scuff-vs-wear classifier → keypoint symmetry → heatmap and price note. **Data:** weakly labelled OLX photos; ~30 mechanic-inspected bikes for a hit rate. **Demo:** judge uploads any bike photo. **Runs:** server L4 ~0.8 s, ~$0.002. **Moat:** two-wheeler crash-signature labels. **vs existing:** Motoryk sells a $4.99 one-off report [43] → fleet check-in loop; 563 moped/scooter crashes in Jan-Sep 2026 [16].
- **CarTrail** (62) — Live cross-listing check: finds the same car on OLX, Avtoelon and Telegram now, flags price, mileage and seller discrepancies, while a dated archive grows into a history report. **Payer:** banks for collateral (8,000 UZS per API call), dealers, buyers (25,000 UZS). **CV → action:** DINOv3 retrieval → ALIKED/LightGlue on dents, rims, stickers → plate fragments and odometer OCR → match timeline. **Data:** public listing photos, hashed embeddings, hosted in-country [44]. **Demo:** judge pastes a listing screenshot and sees matched crops. **Runs:** T4 embeds once, CPU lookup <1 s, <$0.002 per report. **Moat:** vanished listings cannot be backfilled. **vs existing:** Autoteka uses Avito's own data [45] → cross-platform re-identification over 67,961 OLX car ads [46].
- **Yorliq** (62) — Chinese nameplate decoder feeding Quvvat: plate photo → variant → nominal kWh, flagging listings that claim the wrong pack. **Payer:** used-EV dealers, brokers and banks, inside Quvvat (~15k UZS per plate standalone). **CV → action:** PP-OCRv6 Chinese and Latin → VLM field parse → VIN check digit → spec table → mismatch flag. **Data:** ~10 real cars first (kill if the plate does not identify the pack); 2,000 synthetic plates; hand-built table for 15 models. **Demo:** judge photographs our printed nameplate card. **Runs:** server CPU ~0.3 s, <$0.001. **Moat:** verified-mismatch database. **vs existing:** BYD VIN decoders give plant and date, not kWh [47] → one Atto 3 body ships 49.92, 60.48 or 74.8 kWh [48].
- **Trim Truth** (61) — "Ad vs reality" badge verifying only camera-visible trim claims (charge port vs fuel flap, wheels, sunroof, roof rails, rear camera) on Cobalt LS/LT/LTZ and BYD variants. **Payer:** marketplaces; banks only if a loan officer confirms trim inflation; ~$0.50 per check. **CV → action:** SAM 3 part crops → SigLIP 2 retrieval vs official galleries → checklist → mismatch flag. **Data:** UzAuto and BYD galleries, 1,000 listing photos, 50 own cars. **Demo:** judge photographs their own car; model, equipment and trim appear. **Runs:** browser retrieval, server ~1.5 s, ~$0.004. **Moat:** local trim taxonomy. **vs existing:** Auto.ru grades condition [49] → trim truth; Chinese brands took 17.8% of Jan-Jul 2026 sales [50].
- **WearTruth (+TaxiGhost)** (59) — Mileage band from interior wear (steering wheel, pedals, bolster, gear knob) vs the odometer, plus a "commercial use" class from taxi decal ghosts and roof-lamp marks. **Payer:** auto-loan banks and trade-in desks via API, 8,000 UZS per check. **CV → action:** DINOv3-S zone embeddings → quantile regression → interval; abstain on covers; service-sticker OCR as an independent anchor. **Data:** 30+ GPS-logged disposals from a taxi or leasing fleet; first count decals on 100 Tashkent taxis and drop ghosts if under 30%. **Demo:** judge photographs their wheel and pedals and plays guess-the-mileage. **Runs:** in-browser WebGPU, ~zero cost. **Moat:** fleet-labelled wear curves. **vs existing:** OdoShield and AI Vahan [51] → local labels; 576k taxi drivers [26].
- **WeldTell** (58) — Inspector heat map that separates factory spot welds and seam sealer from repair welds on one repeatable region (door-jamb sealer or strut-tower welds) of two models. **Payer:** avtopodbor firms and dealers, ~20,000 UZS per car. **CV → action:** one-class anomaly detection (anomalib PatchCore) on factory references [52] → abstain on dirt or undercoat → flagged areas for a human. **Data:** factory-new references at a sponsor dealer; 40 labelled panels; AUC reported. **Demo:** judge photographs our factory vs repaired sample jambs. **Runs:** browser classifier, CPU server ~0.3 s per patch. **Moat:** per-model references need dealer access. **vs existing:** vendor guides list weld tells and rate AI "partial" [53] → phone-grade triage.
- **DateCode Detective** (57) — Seller-side originality certificate: glass, seat-belt and VIN-plate date codes consistent with build year support the asking price; tyre age is the live demo hook. **Payer:** dealers and trade-in desks, ~20,000 UZS per car. **CV → action:** OWLv2 locates stamps → raking-torch frames → PP-OCRv6 or PaddleOCR-VL → per-maker decoders → timeline vs VIN year. **Data:** 150+ real stamps; per-source read-rate table including "unreadable". **Demo:** judge photographs any tyre sidewall in the car park. **Runs:** tyre OCR in browser; hard stamps to a VLM at ~$0.0003 per photo. **Moat:** decoder tables for glass makers seen locally. **vs existing:** Anyline reads tyre DOT codes [54] → every dated part; PP-OCRv6 claims gains on tyre prints [55].
- **RepaintRadar** (55) — Triage before the gauge: a 20-second phone-torch sweep per panel ranks the three panels worth checking with a paint gauge; abstains on dark metallics, plastic and glare. **Payer:** avtopodbor firms and trade-in desks, 15,000 UZS per car; buyers 5,000 UZS. **CV → action:** highlight sharpness (orange peel) + Lab delta vs neighbour panel + seal overspray (SAM 2.1) → repaint probability. **Data:** cheap-gauge labels on 40 cars × 12 panels; held-out-car AUC. **Demo:** judge sweeps a torch over a glossy door. **Runs:** browser DINOv3-S, ~1 s per panel. **Moat:** gauge-labelled local finishes. **vs existing:** Oto Dedektif claims repaint flags [56] → publish a head-to-head; Heydealer found unrecorded repairs on ~38% of 15,236 cars [57].

### Insurers

- **WashScan** (66) — see Top picks.
- **BumperMatch** (58) — Metric damage-geometry report for adjusters: plate-scaled height, width and shape of damage on both cars; flags only pairings impossible beyond measured error; inverse hit-and-run mode suggests the other vehicle class. **Payer:** OSAGO claims teams and independent experts, ~$0.30 per two-car check. **CV → action:** 520 × 110 mm plate scale [58] + MoGe-2 heights + SAM 3 damage masks → bumper catalogue → plausible? **Data:** tape-measured catalogue of ~25 models; tape vs MoGe-2 error on 30 cars. **Demo:** judge photographs a scratch near any plate and picks the other car. **Runs:** server <3 s, ~$0.001. **Moat:** local height catalogue; Cobalt took 30.1% of 2025 sales [59]. **vs existing:** CCC patent, expert tools [60] → automatic screening.
- **Alibi (+ProofShot)** (58) — Photo liveness for Telegram-bot claims: "taken now" plus shared live location, then physics checks (sun elevation vs shadows, sky and wet road vs observed weather, flash echo and parallax vs screen replays). **Payer:** OSAGO/KASKO claims desks, $0.05-0.20 per claim. **CV → action:** RF-DETR car + shadow masks + pvlib sun geometry + SigLIP 2 weather → fast-track, hold or retake; abstains often. **Data:** 300 own timed photos plus attacks. **Demo:** judge photographs their car, claims a false time, sees the sun overlay reject it. **Runs:** server CPU ~1.5 s, ~$0.002. **Moat:** physics survives better generators. **vs existing:** Amped's manual SunCalc check [61] → automatic; humans catch only 50% of manipulated damage photos [62].
- **ScratchPrint** (57) — SAME-vs-NEW damage for taxi, rental and car-sharing shift handovers and an insurer's inception-vs-claim check: re-identifies the same physical scratch in new photos. **Payer:** fleets per handover; insurers ~$0.25 per claim; cross-insurer registry later via the Guarantee Fund. **CV → action:** SAM 2.1 panel warp → RF-DETR-Seg damage → ALIKED/XFeat + LightGlue geometry match → same, new or insufficient. **Data:** AutoDamageIQ (CC BY), ~40 self-shot revisit pairs. **Demo:** judge photographs a pre-staged dent twice, hours apart. **Runs:** Modal T4 ~2 s, ~$0.001. **Moat:** embedding-only registry grows with members. **vs existing:** Inspektlabs, Click-Ins and Ravin sell baselines [63] → damage-geometry re-ID; one appraiser reused a photo in 170 claims [64].
- **Podstava Radar** (53) — Measurement-only braking-and-headway timeline from any dashcam clip before a rear-end crash: time-to-contact, brake-light timing and a "visible reason to brake?" flag for a human; no scam verdict. **Payer:** taxi parks defending drivers, ~$1 per car-month; insurers ~$0.50 per clip. **CV → action:** RF-DETR + trackers → optical-expansion time-to-contact → brake-light classifier → dispute report. **Data:** DoTA, CCD and Nexar clips, CARLA scenes; validate against GPS on ~5 drives. **Demo:** judge uploads a dashcam clip and sees the deceleration chart. **Runs:** T4 ~5 s per 15 s clip, ~$0.001. **Moat:** local scenario library. **vs existing:** GoFleet markets brake-check flags without validation [65] → open, measured timeline; interview two parks first.
- **LemonBack** (52) — Seller-paid, insurer-backed 30-day buyback guarantee covering only what a dated 10-minute capture proves (undisclosed repaint or repair, mileage vs service records), buying back at ~95% of trade-in value. **Payer:** private sellers ~300,000 UZS; insurer carries risk, we take commission. **CV → action:** bolt, gap, lamp and wear modules → rules-table price → decline uncertain cars. **Data:** 40 captured cars with inspector verdicts; break-even claim-rate table. **Demo:** live capture on the judge's phone; toggle a defect, watch the premium move. **Runs:** server ~3 s, ~$0.01 per car. **Moat:** loss data from issued guarantees. **vs existing:** 2009 Vehicle Repurchase Guaranty [66] and Carro [67] → photo-priced; KASKO premiums fell 47% in 2025 [33].
- **EuroScene** (50) — Two-phone accident notice: both drivers film a 30-second walk-around, plates are read, damage zones mapped and a pre-filled notification signed by QR handshake; no fault suggestion. **Payer:** Insurance Guarantee Fund or NAPP pilot and ride-hailing fleets, ~$0.40 per notice. **CV → action:** open-image-models plate detector + fine-tuned fast-plate-ocr → SAM 3 damage zones → form fill. **Data:** synthetic Uzbek plates (01 A 123 BC, 01 123 ABC); real plates at night and in rain. **Demo:** judge and a neighbour film a toy-car scene and receive the notice. **Runs:** server ~6 s, ~$0.01. **Moat:** accepted evidence format. **vs existing:** Ukraine's Diia accident report [68] → video-first, attested; the 2026 reform gave the Guarantee Fund adjuster functions [69].

## Shared build kit

- **Inference split.** Put the high-volume step in the browser: full WebGPU reaches 85.72% of global browsers, iOS Safari 26+ included, Firefox for Android excluded [243]; use Transformers.js v4 or ONNX Runtime Web (4 GB WebAssembly ceiling) [244][245] with a server fallback. No phone-browser FPS has been published, so measure on three phones and quote the hardware.
- **Licence-clean models.** RF-DETR Nano-Large and Seg (Apache-2.0; Nano 2.3 ms on a T4) [39], D-FINE-N (15 MB) [246], SAM 2.1 and EdgeTAM (Apache-2.0), SAM 3 (SAM License, gated) [247], DA3 Small/Metric and MoGe-2 (MIT) [248], SigLIP 2, Qwen3.5, Qwen3-VL and Gemma 4 (Apache-2.0), PP-OCRv6 [249], fast-plate-ocr (MIT) [250], MediaPipe.
- **Licence traps.** YOLO26 is AGPL-3.0 and any SaaS use needs an Enterprise licence [251]; DEIMv2 switched to non-commercial [252]; Depth Pro, UniDepth, MASt3R, DA3-Large/Giant and the original VGGT are non-commercial; BoxMOT and OpenALPR are AGPL, Nomeroff-Net GPL, AlbumentationsX AGPL [253].
- **Uzbek plates.** Formats 01 A 123 BC and 01 123 ABC, 520 × 110 mm white reflective [58]; no open Uzbek set or maintained CIS plate generator exists [254]. Render plates in the right fonts with perspective, blur, glare and dust, fine-tune fast-plate-ocr, then validate on the 500-image Central Asia set (CC BY 4.0) [98] and ~100 own dusk shots.
- **Night, rain, dust.** Edit inside masks with FLUX.2 klein 4B [255] or Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 [256] (both Apache-2.0), but no generative edits on plate glyphs; imagecorruptions (Apache-2.0) has fog and frost but no night, rain or dust, so write photometric transforms [257]; Cosmos-Transfer2.5 needs ~65 GB VRAM [258]; evaluate on ACDC [259], ZOD (CC BY-SA, commercial use allowed) [260] and a small real local set.
- **Cost per inspection.** Claude Haiku 5.5 costs about $0.00031 per 1000 × 1000 photo, so 12 photos ≈ $0.004; Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite ≈ $0.014 for the same set [261][262]; a detector on a Modal T4 costs ≈ $0.0000082 per image, but the default 60 s idle window bills ≈ $0.0098 per cold hit [263]. Quote API + compute + capture minutes per inspection.
- **Judge-proof hosting.** Static Hugging Face Spaces are free, new compute Spaces need a paid plan, free CPU Spaces sleep after 48 h [264]; Modal gives $30 a month of credit and scales to zero [263]; Roboflow's free tier covers ~80,000 RF-DETR Nano inferences a month with a commercial licence [265]. Pre-warm before your slot, print a QR code, ship sample files for judges without a car.
- **Data declaration and privacy.** The brief demands source and generation method for all data. Avoid non-commercial sets such as DMD and MVTec AD [266][267]; AutoDamageIQ is CC BY but merged, so check upstream licences [268]. Uzbek law keeps citizens' personal data on servers in Uzbekistan [44]: blur plates and faces or process on-device.
- **No AI-image detection claims.** Claude cannot tell whether an image is AI-generated [269] and the strongest open forensic models (TruFor, B-Free) are non-commercial [270]; use provenance (c2pa-rs) [271], capture-time challenges and human review.
- **Edge and proof.** An edge node costs ~$505 (Raspberry Pi 5 16 GB at $305 plus AI HAT+ 2 at $200) [272][144]; every measuring idea needs a reference table on stage (tape, caliper, paint gauge, weighbridge ticket) with error bars for day, dusk and rain.
